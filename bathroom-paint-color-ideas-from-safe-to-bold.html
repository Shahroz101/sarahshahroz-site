12 Bathroom Paint Colors, From Totally Safe to Genuinely Bold

From dramatic black to crisp white — a color for every risk tolerance and every amount of natural light.

2026-08-15 · 4 min read

Bathroom paint is one of the cheapest, fastest ways to completely change how the whole room feels.

It's also one of the easiest colors to get wrong, since a small, enclosed space reads color more intensely than almost any other room in the house.

These 12 options cover the full range, from totally safe to genuinely bold.

A Couple of Pro Tips Before You Commit

Test a sample patch under your bathroom's actual lighting. This matters more here than in almost any other room, since bathroom lighting varies so widely from fixture to fixture.

A smaller, darker bathroom generally does better with a lighter color, while a larger one with good natural light can handle something bolder without feeling closed-in.

Go Paint That Bathroom

None of these 12 colors is universally correct. The right one depends on your room's size, light, and the mood you're actually going for.

Whichever you choose, a fresh coat of paint remains one of the fastest, most affordable ways to transform the whole room.
