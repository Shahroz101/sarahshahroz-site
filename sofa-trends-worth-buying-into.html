20 Sofa Trends Worth Knowing, and Which Ones Actually Last

Curved, bouclé, modular, velvet, reclining — 20 current sofa trends sorted into lasting investments and ones worth a closer look first.

2026-10-18 · 5 min read

Sofa trends move slower than most other home categories, mostly because nobody wants to replace a sofa every year.

That makes the "is this worth it" question more important here than almost anywhere else in the house.

This sorts 20 current sofa trends into what's a real long-term shift and what's likely to feel dated sooner than expected.

20 Sofa Trends Sorted Out

1. Wood-Trimmed Curved Sofas

A curved silhouette wrapped in a band of exposed wood, at the base or along the front rail, turns a sofa into more of a furniture piece than a fabric block.

It's a detail that ages well because it reads as craftsmanship rather than a styling trick.

Which Trends Are Actually Worth Following

Modular sectionals, wood-framed sofas, cognac leather, and classic slipcovers are the safest long-term bets here, since their appeal rests on genuine function or proven durability rather than a specific aesthetic moment.

Curved shapes, in nearly every version on this list, have held on long enough now to count as a real shift rather than a trend.

Printed sofas and the boldest pastel curves deserve the most scrutiny, since both depend heavily on personal taste holding up over time, not just the trend itself.
